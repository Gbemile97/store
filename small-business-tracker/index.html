Questions

FAQ

Do I need Microsoft Excel?

No. It works in free Google Sheets on a laptop or phone. Upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets.

I'm not good with spreadsheets. Can I use it?

Yes. You only type in white cells and pick from dropdowns. All the formulas are already done, and the guide walks you through setup step by step.

I'm not in Nigeria. Will it work for me?

Yes. Choose your currency in Settings (₦, $, £, €, GH₵, KSh, R or CA$) and all the money headings change to match.

Can I use it for a service business?

Yes. Add each service as a "product" with your material cost and price. You'll still see revenue, profit and your best services.

Is this accounting software?

No. It's a simple tracker for day-to-day sales, stock and profit. For tax filing or audited accounts, speak to an accountant.

How do I get the files?

Right after payment on Selar you'll get a download with the tracker and the quick-start guide.
