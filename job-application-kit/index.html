FAQ

What is an ATS-friendly CV?

A CV with a simple, one-column layout and standard headings that screening software can read correctly, plus the right keywords for the job. The template handles the layout; the playbook shows you how to add the right keywords.

Will this work for remote or international jobs?

Yes. The playbook includes a section on what to remove from local-style CVs (like photos, age and marital status) when applying internationally.

Do I need Microsoft Word?

No. Everything works in free Google Docs and Google Sheets, on a laptop or phone.

Do I need to pay for ChatGPT?

No. The prompts work with free versions of ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. You can also do everything without AI using the playbook.

Does this guarantee me a job?

No, and nobody honest can promise that. The kit removes the common reasons good candidates get filtered out and helps you present your real experience properly.
