Questions

FAQ

Do I need Microsoft Excel?

No. It works in free Google Sheets. Upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets.

I'm not in Nigeria. Will it work for me?

Yes. Pick your home currency in Settings and set the exchange rates for the currencies you're paid in. Add more currencies to the table if you need them.

Does it connect to Upwork or my bank?

No. You type in each payment yourself, which takes under a minute. Nothing is shared with any platform.

What about changing platform fees?

Set each platform's fee in Settings, and override it on any single payment if that contract had a different fee.

Is this tax advice?

No. The tax set-aside is a simple percentage you choose. Speak to a tax adviser about what you actually owe.

How do I get the files?

Right after payment on Selar you'll get a download with the tracker and the quick-start guide.
